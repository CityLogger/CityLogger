Hello App Review,

Thank you for the review and clear guidance. We have addressed each issue in the replacement build 1.0 ([BUILD NUMBER]).

**Guideline 2.1(a) — Review access**

We added a dedicated, email-confirmed review account in the App Review Information User name and Password fields. It does not use MFA or require access to an email inbox. The account is pre-populated with 10 visited cities across multiple countries and continents, varied category ratings and dates, notes, five Want to Visit cities, two personalised lists, a custom ranking and yearly-goal progress.

To sign in, open **Profile → Create account or sign in → I already have an account** and use the credentials supplied in App Review Information. Detailed feature-navigation instructions are included in Review Notes.

**Guideline 2.3.6 — Accurate Age Rating metadata**

CityLogger does not provide Parental Controls or Age Assurance. We corrected the Age Rating questionnaire and set Age Assurance and Parental Controls to **None**.

**Guideline 1.5 — Support URL**

We replaced the previous Support URL with `https://citylogger.app/support`. The page contains a working support email address, account and sign-in help, data export and deletion guidance, privacy-request information and problem-reporting instructions. The support page is also linked from the app’s Profile section.

We also completed the public Privacy Policy and Terms and verified their in-app links.

Thank you for reviewing the updated submission.
